1. Purpose 
This Code of Conduct defines the expectations for team behavior, communication, and 
responsibilities. All team members agree to follow these rules to ensure a productive and respectful  
working environment. 
2. Team Roles 
• Scrum Master (Susan): Facilitates meetings and ensures Scrum principles are followed. 
• Product Owner(Vatsal): Oversees task tracking, documentation, and progress on the Git 
Hub. 
3. Team Meetings 
• Team meetings will occur 
• Meetings should last no longer than 15 minutes for standups. 
• Attendance is mandatory; if absent, notify the team in advance. 
4. Task Responsibilities 
• All assigned tasks must be completed by the deadline. 
• Team members are responsible for updating task progress on the Git Hub. 
• If a member cannot complete a task, they must communicate this immediately. 
•  
5. Communication 
• Use [insert tool: Google Meet / WhatsApp / Zoom] for updates and discussions. 
• Be respectful and professional in all communications. 
• Resolve conflicts politely within the team before escalating. 
6. Participation & Accountability 
• Every team member is expected to contribute fairly. 
• Non-participation may be addressed by the team or escalated to the teaching staff if 
necessary. 
• Collaboration is key — all work should be shared and visible to the team. 
7. Lab Attendance 
• All members should attend lab sessions together. 
• Lab time is part of teamwork and progress tracking. 
8. Agreement 
By signing below, each team member agrees to follow this Code of Conduct and contribute to the
